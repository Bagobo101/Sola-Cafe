Why nighttime

A café that runs on your clock, not the sun's.

Sola Cafe started as a 6AM pop-up cart outside a High School, pouring coffee for Students and teachers coming off. Enough of them asked for a place to sit down that we found one — a narrow storefront two doors from the print shop, open from 6AM to 6PM, Monday to Friday.

We roast in small batches during the day so the beans are ready by the time our door opens. Everything on the bar is built for people who are mid-shift, not winding down: strong where it needs to be strong, quiet where it needs to be quiet, and never rushing you out before you're ready to go.

The room seats 8. Half the tables have outlets. All of them have good light for reading, and none of them have music loud enough to interrupt a conversation at 1AM.
