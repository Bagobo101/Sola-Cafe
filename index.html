Why daytime

A Café that runs on the school week, not the clock.

Sola Café started with a simple idea: to serve freshly brewed coffee to students and teachers beginning their day. We found the perfect spot in a rented stall right in front of Marciano High School, where we now welcome customers from 6AM to 6PM, Monday to Friday.

We roast in small batches overnight so the beans are ready by the time our door opens at 6AM. Everything on the Counter and in the kitchen is built for people starting their day: strong coffee, a real breakfast, and rice meals that hold you until dinner.

The room seats 8. Half the tables have toys to play with and books to read. All of them have good light for reading, and the kitchen keeps serving right up until we close the door at 6PM.
