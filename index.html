Why daytime

A café that runs on the school week, not the clock.

Sola Cafe started as a 6AM pop-up cart outside a high school, pouring coffee for students and teachers coming in. Enough of them asked for a place to sit down that we found one — a narrow storefront two doors from the print shop, open from 6AM to 6PM, Monday to Friday.

We roast in small batches overnight so the beans are ready by the time our door opens at 6AM. Everything on the bar and in the kitchen is built for people starting their day: strong coffee, a real breakfast, and rice meals that hold you until dinner.

The room seats 8. Half the tables have outlets. All of them have good light for reading, and the kitchen keeps serving right up until we close the door at 6PM.
